# Unit modelling briefs — fantasy civ rosters (27 models)

One brief per unit that needs a model, for the six fantasy civs in
`docs/plans/fantasy-civs.md`. Founders and Gatherers keep their existing
models and are not re-briefed here.

Status: DESIGN ONLY — no art has been generated; the civ pivot itself is
still Proposed
(`decisions/D-20260818-fantasy-civs-supersede-the-historical-frame.md`).

## Ground rules (apply to every brief below)

- **Budget:** ~300 triangles per soldier is the standing budget (D-081).
  Where a brief exceeds it, the overage is stated and justified —
  always a unit with few instances per squad, so the squad's total
  triangle count stays at or below a 300×40 line squad.
- **Silhouette first.** Every brief lists silhouette priorities in
  order; if the budget runs out, cut from the bottom of that list,
  never the top. A unit must read at RTS zoom among thousands.
- **Procedural build.** Models are generated by committed Python
  driving Blender headless (D-081): describe every part as primitives
  (boxes, cylinders, lathes, hulls, mirrored pairs). Briefs list a
  parts kit for that reason. Watch winding — an inside-out part lights
  wrong and nothing fails (the M7 `box()` lesson).
- **Scale baseline:** one human soldier = **1.0 H** (≈1.8 m). All
  heights below are in H. Big models change `formation_spacing` in
  data, not the mesh's origin: every model stands on y=0, origin at
  feet-centre.
- **Player colour:** ownership tint is applied per instance. Each brief
  names its **tint zones** (cloth, banners, shield faces — the parts
  that take player colour) and its **fixed zones** (skin, bone, wood,
  iron — authored material colours; remember the sRGB pre-compensation
  rule in `art/lib/bake.py`, D-100). Tint zones must be big enough to
  read at zoom: at least ~15% of the visible silhouette.
- **Animation:** vertex-animation texture, phase derived (D-082). Each
  unit needs **idle / walk / attack** loops; briefs give the attack
  beat only, since idle and walk follow the body type. No ragdolls, no
  transparency, no particles — anything "atmospheric" is geometry.
- **Engines:** one instance = one complete engine WITH its crew
  sculpted on. There is no crew-pushes-object mechanic; a "battery" is
  squad_size instances of the whole model.

## Base-body kit (build once, reuse everywhere)

| base | used by | notes |
|---|---|---|
| **human base** (1.0 H, ~120 tris naked) | Hillkin, all Gildedreach foot | the existing archetype body, reused |
| **elf base** (1.05 H, slender, ~120 tris) | all Thornwood foot | narrower shoulders, longer neck — the vertical read |
| **dwarf base** (0.75 H, 1.4× width, ~120 tris) | all Emberdeep foot + crews | half the height, twice the width of the elf |
| **skeleton base** (0.95 H, gaunt, ~140 tris) | all Gravesworn foot | needs visible gaps: ribcage notch, stick limbs — thinness IS the read |
| **giant base** (2.5 H, ~300 tris) | all Stoneblood giants | hunched neutral pose so height reads as mass, not a pole |
| **centaur base** (1.4 H at head, ~220 tris) | all Windmarch | horse barrel + human torso; four legs animate as trot |
| **horse** (~160 tris) | Hired Outriders | the deliberately ordinary mount |
| **stag** (~180 tris + antlers) | Stag Riders | antlers are the point — budget them first |
| **timber kit** (beams, wheels, frames) | all four engines | shared lathes/boxes; each engine is an assembly of it |

## STONEBLOOD (giant-kin)

### 1. `hillkin_clubs` — Hillkin Clubs (line, 24/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.0 H, 300 tris (human base).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) two-handed club held diagonally across
  the body, head-high — the weapon is half the read; (2) squat stance,
  wide feet; (3) fur cloak mass rounding the shoulders.
- **Build:** human base + tapered-cylinder club with knob end + hull
  cloak over shoulders + simple hood.
- **Tint zones:** cloak lining and a waist sash. **Fixed:** fur
  (dark brown), club (raw wood), skin.
- **Attack beat:** slow full-body overhead swing, weight shifting to
  the front foot — heavier and slower than any other levy's attack.
- **Don't:** no shield — bare second hand on the club distinguishes
  them from every shielded levy at a glance.

### 2. `young_giants` — Young Giants (heavy line, 8/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 2.5 H, **450 tris** (over budget; 8 instances —
  squad total 3,600 tris, a tenth of a line squad).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) sheer height and shoulder mass —
  hunched, head jutting forward, arms hanging past mid-thigh; (2)
  tree-trunk club, dragged head-down at rest, not shouldered; (3) bare
  head — no helmet anywhere on this unit.
- **Build:** giant base + club (tapered cylinder with root-ball end) +
  rope-wrapped forearms (torus rings) + hide kilt.
- **Tint zones:** the hide kilt and a rope shoulder-band. **Fixed:**
  grey-tan skin, wood.
- **Attack beat:** one-armed sideways sweep at knee height — reads as
  clearing men away, distinct from the Hillkin's overhead chop.
- **Don't:** no armour plates — armour would converge on the dwarf
  vanguard's read. A giant is skin, hide and wood.

### 3. `cragthrowers` — Cragthrowers (ranged, 12/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 2.4 H, **420 tris**.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) boulder held overhead two-handed at
  full extension — the throw pose IS the identity, bake it into idle
  as "hefting"; (2) a sling-bag of rocks on the hip; (3) same hunched
  giant frame as the Young Giants.
- **Build:** giant base + icosphere-ish hull boulder + hip bag (hull) +
  broad belt.
- **Tint zones:** the belt and bag flap. **Fixed:** skin, stone
  (mid-grey — must contrast with the skin tone so the boulder reads).
- **Attack beat:** lean back, both arms over head, whole-torso pitch
  forward. The release must be readable in one silhouette frame.
- **Don't:** no bow, no thrown-spear pose — nothing that could be
  mistaken for a human archer scaled up.

### 4. `gatebreakers` — Gatebreakers ★ (siege, 6/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 2.8 H — **the tallest thing in the game** —
  **500 tris** (6 instances).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) head-down, forward-leaning walk, like a
  battering ram deciding to be a person; (2) a stone maul the size of a
  door, dragged behind on the ground; (3) stone plates lashed to
  shoulders and forearms — the one giant with "armour", and it is
  masonry, not metal.
- **Build:** giant base scaled up + box-slab maul on a thick haft +
  angular hull plates (visibly chipped: irregular hulls, not clean
  boxes) + drag chains (thin cylinders, static).
- **Tint zones:** a banner-cloth tied to the maul haft — small but
  high-contrast; plus the kilt. **Fixed:** skin, stone plates (match
  cliff-rock grey so the "living siege engine" read lands).
- **Attack beat:** two-handed maul lift and vertical drop, whole body
  rising then collapsing into the blow — a demolition beat, not a
  combat swing.
- **Don't:** never upright posture; the lean is what separates them
  from Young Giants at distance.

## GRAVESWORN (deathless court)

### 5. `corpse_levy` — Corpse Levy (line, 48/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 0.95 H, **260 tris** — deliberately the cheapest
  model in the game; there are 48 of them per squad.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) gaunt broken outline — stick limbs,
  caved chest, head hung; (2) a rusted blade held low and wrong, tip
  near the ground; (3) rag tatters breaking every straight edge.
- **Build:** skeleton base + short sword (plain box blade) + 3–4 rag
  strips (single-sided… no: double-faced thin hulls — no transparency
  available, so rags are geometry).
- **Tint zones:** one chest rag / tabard remnant. **Fixed:** bone
  (warm off-white, not pure white), rust, grave-cloth grey.
- **Attack beat:** graceless single hack, no wind-up recovery — it
  swings like something that doesn't protect itself, because it
  doesn't.
- **Don't:** no two identical-looking poses side by side if the
  generator supports variants; if not, asymmetric gear (one pauldron,
  one bare arm) fakes irregularity in a crowd.

### 6. `bone_pikes` — Bone Pikes (anti-cavalry, 40/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 0.95 H, 280 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) pike at 60°, twice the soldier's
  height — the squad must read as a hedge of points; (2) remnant
  cuirass and open helm, old discipline on a dead body; (3) same gaunt
  frame beneath.
- **Build:** skeleton base + long thin cylinder pike with leaf head +
  breastplate hull + half-helm.
- **Tint zones:** a pennon rag below the pike head — 40 pennons make
  the squad's ownership read as a field of flags. **Fixed:** bone,
  blackened iron.
- **Attack beat:** two-handed pike thrust from the hip, minimal body
  motion — mechanical, disciplined, dead.
- **Don't:** pikes must NOT wave in idle; a rigid hedge against the
  levy's slump is the discipline-vs-decay contrast inside the civ.

### 7. `barrow_shades` — Barrow Shades ★ (flanker, 20/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.05 H (taller than the other dead), 280 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) a single long cloak falling to a point
  below the knees — the whole body is one tapering shape; (2) deep
  hood, no visible face geometry at all; (3) a long knife held
  reversed, tight to the body.
- **Build:** skeleton base largely HIDDEN under a cloak hull (this is
  the trick: the cloak is the model, ~60% of the tris); hood; reversed
  dagger.
- **Tint zones:** cloak inner lining, visible at the front opening —
  the one flash of player colour on an otherwise dark shape. **Fixed:**
  near-black cloak (darkest material in the roster; darkness does the
  ghost-work that transparency can't).
- **Attack beat:** a lunge-step with the knife arm unfolding — fast,
  low, forward; the walk cycle should be a glide with minimal bob.
- **Don't:** no glow, no transparency, no trailing wisps — the deleted
  ghost shader is not coming back for this unit. Shape and darkness
  only.

### 8. `carrion_hurler` — Carrion Hurler (engine, 3/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 2.2 H at the frame's top, length 2.5 H, **600
  tris** including two crew (3 instances/squad).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) crooked gallows A-frame with a long
  throwing arm — deliberately asymmetric, nothing square; (2) the
  sling-bundle at the arm's end (a wrapped corpse-shape, kept
  abstract); (3) two skeleton crew fused to the winch positions.
- **Build:** timber kit beams set 3–5° off true + bone reinforcements
  (rib-curve cylinders) + two reduced skeleton bases (~100 tris each,
  attached, animated with the machine) + solid wheels.
- **Tint zones:** a hanging banner off the frame's rear. **Fixed:**
  grey timber, bone, dark iron.
- **Attack beat:** arm swings from cocked to released over ~0.5 s, crew
  crank pose between shots; the machine rocks on its wheels at release.
- **Don't:** no projectile in the model — missiles are the game's
  problem, not the mesh's. The sling hangs empty after release within
  the loop.

## THORNWOOD (sylvan elves)

### 9. `glade_wardens` — Glade Wardens (line, 28/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.05 H, 300 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) tall thin vertical: slender body, high
  pointed helm, upright leaf-bladed spear; (2) a slim oval shield worn
  on the arm, not squared to the front; (3) knee-length coat splitting
  over the legs.
- **Build:** elf base + spear (cylinder + flattened leaf-hull head) +
  oval shield + tall conical helm with a back-swept point.
- **Tint zones:** shield face and coat hem. **Fixed:** bronze-green
  armour trim, pale wood.
- **Attack beat:** short precise underarm spear thrust, shield rolling
  forward — economical, no lunge.
- **Don't:** the helm point must not read as the Gravesworn hood at
  zoom — keep it swept BACK where the Shade's tapers DOWN.

### 10. `heartbow_archers` — Heartbow Archers (ranged, 26/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.05 H, 300 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) a tall recurve bow, its top limb above
  the head — bow height is the range statement; (2) asymmetric stance,
  side-on; (3) hip quiver fanning arrow fletchings.
- **Build:** elf base + recurve bow (three-segment curve of thin
  boxes) + quiver cylinder + 4–5 fletching quads as thin hulls +
  hooded half-cloak.
- **Tint zones:** the half-cloak and fletchings — coloured fletchings
  make massed volleys legible as YOURS. **Fixed:** heartwood
  red-brown bow (the civ's signature material), green-grey cloth.
- **Attack beat:** full draw to anchor, held one beat, loose and arm
  follow-through. The draw must be visible in silhouette (elbow high).
- **Don't:** never front-on stance; side-on is what separates archer
  from warden when both are thin and vertical.

### 11. `stag_riders` — Stag Riders (light cavalry, 14/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** rider+mount 1.5 H at antler tip, **440 tris**
  (mounted unit; 14 instances).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) **antlers** — wide, forward-swept,
  budgeted before anything else on the model; (2) the stag's straight
  level back and thin legs (vs the horse's bulk); (3) rider leaning
  forward with a short spear couched low.
- **Build:** stag base (antlers as mirrored 3-branch cylinder trees) +
  reduced elf rider (~140 tris, legs merged to the flanks) + short
  spear + saddle-cloth.
- **Tint zones:** saddle-cloth — large and readable, the primary tint
  surface. **Fixed:** russet stag hide, white rump patch (a real
  deer-read at distance), rider in warden colours.
- **Attack beat:** stag rears slightly as the rider thrusts down-and-
  forward; walk cycle is a springing trot with more vertical bounce
  than any horse.
- **Don't:** antlers must never merge visually with a spear held high —
  keep the rider's weapon LOW at all times.

### 12. `dawnfletch_sentinels` — Dawnfletch Sentinels ★ (long-range, 16/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.05 H kneeling ≈ 0.8 H tall, 320 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) the greatbow: taller than the soldier
  even kneeling, a full-height arc; (2) the angled stake planted ahead
  — the two-element silhouette (soldier + stake) no other unit has;
  (3) kneeling brace pose as the BASE stance.
- **Build:** elf base posed kneeling + greatbow (long thin curve, ~2 H
  tall) + stake (angled box with a forked top) + back quiver.
- **Tint zones:** a tall thin banner-ribbon tied to the stake.
  **Fixed:** heartwood bow, pale stake wood.
- **Attack beat:** the whole body rocks back to draw — drawing this
  bow visibly costs strength — then a long still aim and loose. The
  slowest, most deliberate archery in the game; the animation should
  say so.
- **Don't:** the unit never stands fully upright in any loop; kneeling
  is its shape.

## WINDMARCH (centaur clans)

All four are the centaur base. The FOUR-LEGGED SILHOUETTE is the civ's
read; individual units differ by torso gear and weapon line only, so
those differences must be strong.

### 13. `colt_levy` — Colt Levy (line, 28/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.4 H at head, **400 tris** (centaur base).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) spear held strictly VERTICAL at rest —
  28 verticals over horse-bodies reads as a line of infantry that
  happens to be centaurs; (2) round hide shield on the left arm; (3)
  lighter, shorter horse-body than the Lancers.
- **Build:** centaur base + spear + round shield + simple chest strap.
- **Tint zones:** shield face + a rump-blanket. **Fixed:** bay hide,
  tan skin, wood.
- **Attack beat:** overarm downward spear jab with a half-step of the
  forelegs — fighting like infantry from a cavalry body.
- **Don't:** spear never couches horizontal; horizontal is the
  Lancers' line and the two must differ at maximum zoom-out.

### 14. `harriers` — Harriers (screen, 24/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.3 H (visibly smaller/lighter frame), 380 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) javelin raised at 45° in a throwing
  cock — permanently about to throw; (2) no shield, bare torso with a
  single sash; (3) the slightest horse-body in the civ, tail high.
- **Build:** centaur base at 0.92 scale + javelin + 2-javelin quiver on
  the flank + sash.
- **Tint zones:** the sash and javelin streamer. **Fixed:** dun hide.
- **Attack beat:** torso twist and javelin release, forelegs never
  stopping — it throws at the canter; the walk and attack loops should
  share leg motion.
- **Don't:** nothing on this model may read "armoured"; it is the
  fastest-looking and least protected shape in the civ.

### 15. `storm_lancers` — Storm Lancers (shock, 16/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.5 H — the biggest centaur — **430 tris**.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) lance couched HORIZONTAL, projecting a
  full body-length ahead; (2) armoured torso and horse-front: chest
  plates on both bodies; (3) heavy mane and tail masses.
- **Build:** centaur base at 1.07 scale + long lance + torso cuirass +
  horse chest-plate (chamfron optional if budget holds) + tail hull.
- **Tint zones:** lance pennon + a full caparison-strip along the
  horse-body's side — the shock unit carries the most player colour in
  the civ. **Fixed:** dark hide, iron plate.
- **Attack beat:** body drops into the lance as the hind legs drive —
  one committed impact pose, minimal arm motion; the mass does the
  acting.
- **Don't:** never raise the lance vertical (that's the levy); at rest
  it stays horizontal in the crook of the arm.

### 16. `bowriders` — Bowriders ★ (mounted missile, 16/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.4 H, **410 tris**.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) **torso twisted 90–140° at the waist,
  loosing BACKWARDS over the rump** — the Parthian shot is the whole
  identity and must exist in the idle pose, not just the attack; (2)
  short recurve bow (half the elves' height — a horse bow); (3) full
  quivers on both flanks.
- **Build:** centaur base with rotated torso joint + short recurve +
  two flank quivers + fur-trimmed cap.
- **Tint zones:** cap and both quiver flaps. **Fixed:** palomino hide
  (lightest in the civ — the scout reads bright at distance).
- **Attack beat:** the twist deepens, draw, loose, untwist — legs at
  full canter throughout, shared with the walk loop like the Harriers.
- **Don't:** the bow must be visibly SHORT; a tall bow on a centaur
  would collapse the read into "elf on a horse".

## GILDEDREACH (free cities)

The human-baseline civ: five foot/horse units that must look
professional, uniform and PAID. Crisp geometry, matched kit — the
contrast civ for every ragged, bestial or ancient silhouette above.

### 17. `city_watch` — City Watch (line, 30/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.0 H, 300 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) halberd over the shoulder at a uniform
  angle — thirty matched diagonals is the "drilled" read; (2) kettle
  hat (wide flat brim — unique headgear in the game); (3) knee-length
  tabard over mail.
- **Build:** human base + halberd (shaft + axe-hull + rear spike) +
  kettle hat (flattened cone + brim disc) + tabard.
- **Tint zones:** the tabard — the biggest single tint surface on any
  foot unit, city-livery style. **Fixed:** mail grey, wood.
- **Attack beat:** halberd comes off the shoulder into a two-handed
  chop-and-hook — one clean drilled motion.
- **Don't:** no individual variance; this unit's character is that
  every soldier is identical.

### 18. `pike_serjeants` — Pike Serjeants (anti-cavalry, 28/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.0 H, 290 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) pike VERTICAL at rest, longer than the
  Bone Pikes' and dead straight — a tidy forest against their crooked
  hedge; (2) open-faced sallet + breastplate, matched; (3) both hands
  visibly on the shaft.
- **Build:** human base + long pike + sallet + breastplate + rolled
  cloak across the back.
- **Tint zones:** shoulder cords and the rolled cloak. **Fixed:**
  bright steel (the shiniest material in the game's roster — money).
- **Attack beat:** pike drops from vertical to a braced 30° set against
  charge — the SET is the attack; no thrust needed to read.
- **Don't:** never let idle sway the pikes (same rule as Bone Pikes,
  for the opposite reason: these are drilled, those are dead).

### 19. `quarrel_companies` — Quarrel Companies (ranged, 24/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.0 H, 310 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) crossbow held HORIZONTAL at eye line —
  the flat line against every angled bow in the game; (2) a tall
  pavise shield slung square on the back; (3) bolt-case on the belt.
- **Build:** human base + crossbow (stock box + short bow-arms +
  stirrup) + back pavise (rounded-top board) + bolt case.
- **Tint zones:** the pavise face — a heraldic board carried into
  battle backwards; it makes the squad's rear read as a wall of player
  colour. **Fixed:** steel, dark wood.
- **Attack beat:** shoulder, loose, then the reload: foot to stirrup,
  both hands span the string — the reload IS the character animation
  and justifies the slow interval.
- **Don't:** crossbow never angles upward more than ~10°; flat
  trajectory, flat silhouette.

### 20. `hired_outriders` — Hired Outriders (light cavalry, 14/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** rider+horse 1.4 H, **420 tris**.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) an ordinary horse — deliberately the
  baseline mount the stag and centaurs diverge from; (2) rider upright
  with a light sabre; (3) saddlebags (a mercenary carries his pay).
- **Build:** horse base + reduced human rider (~140 tris) + sabre +
  saddlebags + brimmed hat (not a helmet).
- **Tint zones:** saddle-cloth and hat band. **Fixed:** brown horse,
  buff coat.
- **Attack beat:** standing-in-stirrups downward sabre cut on the
  right side.
- **Don't:** no lance, no barding — nothing that promises shock this
  unit doesn't deliver.

### 21. `gilded_sellswords` — Gilded Sellswords ★ (elite heavy, 18/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.05 H (taller via helm crest), **340 tris**.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) full plate: the only closed-helm,
  fully-armoured HUMAN silhouette in the game; (2) greatsword carried
  point-up in both hands; (3) front-rank banner — give every soldier a
  small back-banner so the squad reads as a parade.
- **Build:** human base + plate hulls over every segment + closed helm
  with crest ridge + greatsword + back-banner (pole + stiff pennant).
- **Tint zones:** the back-banner and helm crest. **Fixed:** gold-trimmed
  steel — the trim is authored colour, the "gilded" read, and stays
  gold whatever the player colour is.
- **Attack beat:** a measured two-handed diagonal cut with a step —
  parade-ground perfect, unhurried, expensive.
- **Don't:** the gold trim must be geometry-scale (edging strips), not
  texture detail that vanishes at zoom.

### 22. `contract_engines` — Contract Engines (engine, 4/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 1.4 H tall, 2.2 H long, **560 tris** including two
  crew.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) a horizontal bolt-thrower arm-pair on a
  wheeled frame — a giant crossbow, echoing the Quarrel Companies'
  flat line at building scale; (2) two human crew at the winch and
  trail; (3) neat tool racks — a PROFESSIONAL machine against the
  Hurler's gallows.
- **Build:** timber kit, square and true + bow-arm pair + winch drum +
  two reduced human crew (~110 tris each) + spoked-suggestion wheels
  (solid discs with painted spokes are fine at this size — geometry
  spokes only if budget holds).
- **Tint zones:** a frame-mounted livery board. **Fixed:** clean
  timber, steel fittings.
- **Attack beat:** arms snap forward, frame recoils on its wheels,
  crew re-crank.
- **Don't:** no bolt in the flight groove after release (same rule as
  the Hurler — projectiles are not the mesh's job).

## EMBERDEEP (deep-hold dwarves)

### 23. `hearth_levy` — Hearth Levy (line, 26/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 0.75 H, 300 tris (dwarf base).
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) half the height, twice the width of an
  elf — the proportion IS the civ; (2) round shield nearly as tall as
  the bearer; (3) one-handed axe + braided beard mass on the chest.
- **Build:** dwarf base + big round shield + bearded axe + beard hull
  (a real shape, not a texture) + domed helm with cheek plates.
- **Tint zones:** shield face + helm crest strip. **Fixed:** iron,
  leather, beard (authored per-civ hair colour).
- **Attack beat:** compact axe chop from behind the shield — nothing
  leaves the shield's cover.
- **Don't:** no exposed running pose; even the walk is a stumping,
  planted gait. Speed 2.9 should look like a CHOICE.

### 24. `shieldwall_vanguard` — Shieldwall Vanguard (heavy line, 20/squad)
- **Scale/budget:** 0.78 H, 320 tris.
- **Silhouette, in order:** (1) a TOWER shield covering chin to floor —
  in formation only helms and axe-heads show over the rims, and the
  model must support that read from the front; (2) heavy pauldrons
  squaring the outline into a block; (3) a shorter, thicker axe held
  at the shield's edge.
- **Build:** dwarf base + tower shield (curved plate, floor-touching) +
  pauldron hulls + axe + full-face helm with an eye slot.
- **Tint zones:** a vertical shield-face panel (the wall of player
  colour is the squad's whole look) + a small back-banner on every
  fourth… no — on ALL soldiers or none (no per-soldier variants):
  give all a short back-pole pennant.
- **Fixed:** dark iron, ember-orange helm plume strip (authored — the
  civ's own ember accent, distinct from player tint).
- **Attack beat:** shield-shove then axe hook over the rim — the
  shield is the weapon that moves first.
- **Don't:** the shield never leaves the ground line in idle or walk;
  it slides. Daylight under the shield breaks the wall read.

### 25. `tunnel_quarrels` — Tunnel Quarrels (ranged, 22/squad)
